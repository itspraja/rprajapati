I am an external PhD candidate at the
[SPADE Research Lab, IIT Jodhpur](https://spade-iitj.github.io),
advised by [Prof. Dip Sankar Banerjee](https://sites.google.com/site/dipsankarban/),
and a Researcher in Digital & Scientific HPC at Shell R&D, Bangalore.

My research focuses on **scalable graph algorithms** and **efficient AI systems**,
using graphs as a unifying framework across streaming analytics, distributed
computing, and large-scale LLM deployment on HPC infrastructure.

At Shell, I work on LLM-based systems for engineering workflows — building
retrieval-augmented pipelines, benchmarking model compression techniques,
and optimizing inference on on-prem H100 GPU clusters.
